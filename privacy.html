Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Who is responsible

SvenskaVoca is a Swedish vocabulary app provided by blommorium. For privacy questions and requests, contact swedishvoca@gmail.com.

2. Learning data on your device

Saved and memorised words, achievement dates, recent searches, visited topics, filters and daily selections are stored on your device. There is no account-based learning-history sync. Device backups may include these records. If you enable the optional analytics choices below, new interactions may also be sent to PostHog; we do not upload your existing learning-history database.

3. Your optional analytics choices

On a new installation, all optional choices start off. When you update the app, your existing choices for the remaining categories are preserved. You can use every learning feature without agreeing. We ask again when the collection scope materially changes. Our legal basis for these optional analytics is your consent. In My achievement → Privacy choices, you can choose:

  • Usage analytics: screen visits, active time, vertical scroll milestones, navigation, filters, search lengths and result counts, pronunciation actions, saved/memorised actions and quiz progress/results.
  • Searches and studied words: with usage analytics enabled, search text (limited to 200 characters per event) and the vocabulary word and content identifier associated with an interaction. Searches may be captured after a short typing pause, not only after pressing Search. Do not enter personal or sensitive information in search.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics: independently optional crash stack traces, error types/codes, app/device information and durations and success/failure of vocabulary loading and searches. Fatal crash reports may be sent on the next launch if diagnostics consent still applies. Swift crash messages and system stack frames may be incomplete.

Usage and diagnostic events can include app/OS versions, device type, language and region settings, timestamps, session IDs and a random installation identifier. This identifier lets us associate events from one installation; it is pseudonymous, not a promise of anonymity. Our provider receives IP addresses to deliver requests and may derive approximate country/city information. Device region settings may differ from physical location.

We use this data to understand learning flows, improve features and reliability, and identify errors. We do not request names, email addresses, advertising identifiers or precise GPS location for analytics, sell analytics data, or use it for advertising or cross-company tracking. Raw search text can nevertheless contain identifying information if you type it.

4. Provider, location and retention

PostHog processes selected analytics on our behalf through PostHog Cloud EU. We use the EU ingestion endpoint. Service subprocessors and support access are governed by PostHog's data-processing agreement; where applicable, international transfers rely on the safeguards described there. See PostHog's privacy notice and data-processing agreement.

Starting with version 1.0 (build 3), SvenskaVoca does not collect screen recordings, screen snapshots for session replay, or touch coordinates, even when you choose Allow all. Upgrading removes the old recording preference and discards pending telemetry before analytics restarts. Please update older TestFlight installations to build 3 or later; older builds may still support recording under their earlier consent choices. Recordings previously collected by older builds retain their previously configured 30-day retention period; deletion may take a short time after that period expires.

General analytics events and metadata follow the retention period of our PostHog plan and project settings. PostHog documents a one-year retention period for its Free plan. Expired events are not necessarily deleted immediately and may remain until the provider removes them. You can contact us to request earlier deletion. See PostHog's current retention policy for details.

You can request earlier deletion by emailing swedishvoca@gmail.com with the Analytics ID shown in the app's Privacy choices. We do not intentionally export recordings for indefinite retention.

5. Changing your mind and your rights

Use Allow all, Allow selected, or Decline all in My achievement → Privacy choices. Each action saves your choice and returns you to the app. Declining does not restrict learning features. Withdrawal stops new sharing for the disabled category and discards locally queued telemetry. If local cleanup fails, sharing remains stopped until cleanup succeeds. A request already in transit cannot be recalled. The native crash handler may continue to write a local report until the process exits; reports from outside the current diagnostics-consent period are excluded from upload.

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing or automatically delete previously sent data. To request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy where applicable, email us and include the Analytics ID displayed in Privacy choices. Contact us before deleting the app if you want us to locate earlier events. You may complain to your local data-protection authority; in Sweden this is IMY.

To remove local learning records, use Delete App in iOS Settings. Offload App keeps data. Saved and memorised words can also be removed inside the app. Restoring a device backup may restore earlier records.

6. Content downloads, updates and audio

The app downloads vocabulary from svenska-voca.vercel.app and checks Apple's public App Store information using its bundle identifier and device region. These requests do not include your learning history or searches. The service providers receive network information such as an IP address to serve requests. Update-check dates are stored locally.

Pronunciation uses Apple's AVSpeechSynthesizer. We do not record your voice or access your microphone.

7. Children and changes to this policy

We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. Optional analytics require consent; if you cannot legally consent yourself, leave them off and ask a parent or guardian. Contact us if a child has submitted personal information. We update this page when our practices change and request a new choice for materially expanded optional collection.

8. This website

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising cookies or contact forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement for more information. If you email us, we use the information you send to respond and handle your request.
