Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Your learning records are stored on your device, without account-based learning-history sync.

How do I change my privacy choices?

Open My achievement → Privacy choices in the app. You can change optional usage analytics, searches and studied words, screen recording, and diagnostics. All learning features work without analytics.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

There is no account-based sync. Your device backup may include learning data, depending on your backup settings. Restoring a backup may restore those records.

How do I report an incorrect word or example?

Email the Swedish word, its meaning or example, and what you think should change. A screenshot can help us find the entry.

How can I request deletion of analytics data?

Email us with the Analytics ID shown in the app’s Privacy choices. Contact us before deleting the app so we can locate earlier events. Turning off analytics stops new sharing but does not automatically delete previously sent data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
